Auradio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Operator: Jwoo · support@jwoo.dev

1. Information We Collect

Auradio does not create user accounts. The information below is stored on your device or used temporarily to provide app features.

  • Location (optional): Used only on your device to sort country-based radio lists by proximity. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Favorites, recent stations, Startup Channel, Sleep Timer, and Wake Radio settings: Stored only in on-device storage.
  • Notifications: Local notifications may be used for Wake Radio schedules. They are not sent through a remote push server operated by us.
  • Purchases: Premium purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app only checks whether you have an active entitlement.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Radio Browser API: Used to fetch station lists and stream URLs.
  • Broadcast streams: When you play a station, your device connects to that station’s servers (or the delivery path provided by the broadcaster).
  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing: Used to process in-app purchases.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising tracking.

4. Retention and Deletion

App data is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app removes that data.

5. Children

Auradio is not directed at children under 13.

6. Content and Copyright

The Auradio software, UI, and original icons/banners are copyrighted by Jwoo. Broadcast programming, logos, trademarks, and streams belong to their respective rights holders. Auradio does not claim ownership of broadcast content. Playback may be limited by region or broadcaster policy.

7. Contact
